About Me

I believe a great CMS shouldn't feel like a spreadsheet.

For the past 6 years, I've been working exclusively with WordPress. While many see it simply as a blogging tool, I see it as a powerful, flexible application framework—if built correctly.

I don't just build the front-end that visitors see; I design the back-end experience for the authors and marketers who use it every day. I'm passionate about clean code, web accessibility, and proving that WordPress websites can be blazing fast.

When I'm not writing PHP or React, you can usually find me hiking with my dog or trying to perfect my pour-over coffee recipe.
